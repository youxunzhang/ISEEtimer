FAQ

Does this calculator include both dates?

It can. Turn on the checkbox if you want to count both the start and end dates.

Can I use it for business days?

Yes. The result panel includes a simple weekday-based business day estimate.

Is it free to use?

Yes. The calculator is free online.

Can I use it on mobile?

Yes. The page is mobile friendly.
